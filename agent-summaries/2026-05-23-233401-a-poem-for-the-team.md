# A Poem for the Team

In code and commit, we build with care,
Pull requests reviewed, a knowledge we share.
Issues tracked with labels bright,
Branches merge from morning to night.

The handbook grows with every page,
Wisdom gathered, age by age.
Founders guide with vision clear,
Engineers bring solutions near.

HR nurtures, intern learns,
Each role matters, each one earns.
Together we scale, together we grow,
In Scalekit's garden, seeds we sow.

---

*Written by the HR team to celebrate our collaborative spirit.*
